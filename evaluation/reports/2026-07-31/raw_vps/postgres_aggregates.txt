REAL FINDING: `LLM_MAX_CONCURRENCY` is configured at 25 (not the bottleneck at n=10 concurrent
requests). The latency degradation (20s solo vs 46-82s at 10-concurrent) is genuine CPU contention
on this 4-vCPU host -- the ACIF+retrieval pipeline is CPU-bound, and 10 simultaneous requests
visibly compete for the same 4 cores. This is a real, valid "high-intensity query handling"
(CLAUDE.md §22) capacity finding: at ~10 concurrent real users, per-request latency roughly
triples-to-quadruples versus a single request, on the currently-provisioned hardware.
